As part of the organizing team of the [2022](/talks/2022-hackweek) and [2023](/talks/2023-hackweek) ICESat-2 Hackweeks, I developed and taught tutorials on data discovery and interactive visualization of ICESat-2 data with OpenAltimetry and Google Earth Engine.

The tutorial was later published as a chapter of Project Pythia's open-source *ICESat-2 Cookbook*: Arndt, P. & Roberts, C. (2026). Interactive Visualization with OpenAltimetry and Google Earth Engine. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.21298648](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21298648)
